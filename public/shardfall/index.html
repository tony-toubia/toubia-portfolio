        How to play

        The war

        • Credits come from shards. Harvesters mine them and unload at a Refinery.
        • Power: every structure draws power. Build Reactors. Low power slows production and shuts down turrets and radar.
        • Build from the sidebar. A finished structure waits as READY; place it near your base.
        • Barracks train infantry, the War Factory builds vehicles. The Comms Array turns on the minimap.
        • Two sides. The Vanguard fields armour, artillery and gun turrets; the Hegemony fields fast skimmers, beam tanks, pulse spires and the cloaked Phantom (seen only up close or when it fires).
        • Superweapons: the Orbital Uplink and the Rift Silo charge while powered. When the ring button by the minimap reads READY, use it and pick any explored spot. Everyone sees the warning ring before it lands.
        • Win by destroying every enemy structure (in the campaign: complete the mission objectives). Lose all of yours and it is over.
