What Sets Us Apart

We are not a private equity firm. We are operators with capital.

We combine the long-term thinking of a family office, the financial discipline of private equity, and the focused perspective of operators who have built technology companies from startup to scale.

Our flexible mandate serves one goal — build great technology companies. We bring a proven playbook for scaling recurring-revenue businesses, expanding go-to-market reach, and driving technology-led value creation across our portfolio.

We have a long-term investment horizon with a focus on double-digit multiples of invested capital. We understand that true partnerships are built on shared vision, trust, and respect.

Focus Sectors

Three verticals. Deep conviction.

We concentrate our energy where technology creates compounding competitive advantage — and where our operating expertise delivers differentiated value to portfolio companies.

Tech-Enabled Services

Businesses where proprietary technology amplifies the delivery of high-value services — creating defensible margins, scalable delivery, and measurable client ROI. We look for companies where software is the engine, not the afterthought.

Marketing Services

Data-driven marketing, demand generation, performance media, and CRM platforms for enterprise and mid-market clients. We bring deep roots in the marketing technology landscape and relationships that open doors.

Vertical Software

Purpose-built SaaS and platform businesses serving specific industries — healthcare, legal, real estate, logistics, and more. Vertical software creates sticky, high-NRR businesses that are ripe for buy-and-build expansion.

Investment Criteria

We want to partner with a small, concentrated group of exceptional companies.

Industry

  • Tech-enabled service businesses
  • Marketing & demand-gen platforms
  • Vertical SaaS & workflow software
  • $10B+ addressable markets
  • Fragmented, buy-and-build opportunity
  • AI-augmentable business models

Company

  • $10M–$100M in revenue
  • $3M–$20M in EBITDA or ARR
  • Recurring / subscription revenue base
  • Demonstrated growth track record
  • Limited customer concentration
  • Strong unit economics

Team & Deal

  • Majority or significant minority stake
  • Founder / entrepreneur-led
  • CEO aligned on growth path forward
  • Meaningful management equity rollover
  • Desire for a long-term operating partner
  • Mid-Atlantic or national geography
