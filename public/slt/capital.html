Selective positions where SLT is worth more than the check

PE-style strategic investments in operating businesses and platforms – typically mid-six-figure to seven-figure commitments, concentrated where SLT can create value beyond capital.

How We Invest

Businesses with room to grow, and levers we know how to pull.

TARGET

Target businesses

Operating companies with growth potential and room for sales, technology, or operational improvement – businesses where the next chapter is visible but under-resourced.

VALUE

Value creation

Relationships that open doors, automation that removes cost, operating support that raises the floor, and strategic guidance from people who have run the play before.

RETURN

Return paths

Cash flow, equity appreciation, platform expansion, and strategic exits – multiple ways to win, underwritten honestly.

The Investment Filter

One question decides.

Before any commitment

Can SLT materially increase enterprise value using its network, its technology capability, and its operating experience? If the honest answer is no, the capital stays put.
