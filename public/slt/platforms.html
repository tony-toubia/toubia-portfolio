Pillar 02 · Platforms & Services

Owned platforms that turn manual work into repeatable value

This pillar houses owned or partnered apps, automation products, and scaling services – operating platforms that can be sold, scaled, or used to increase investment value.

Bring Us a Platform Idea

Current Platforms

Two platforms in market. A disciplined intake for what comes next.

SITREP

Situation reporting automation

SitRep helps counties create required situation reports faster, more accurately, and with less manual effort – aggregating disparate sources, standardizing required formats, and supporting emergency management workflows. A wedge into public-sector workflow automation.

SLT SCALE

Back-office scaling partner

Fractional CFO, HR and benefits, payroll and compliance, operations consulting, vendor selection, platform implementation. The operational layer that lets emerging companies scale without immediately building a full internal finance, HR, and operations team.

NEXT

Venture-style intake

Every new platform idea moves through a disciplined scoring model – market size, urgency, buyer clarity, willingness to pay, build complexity, distribution advantage, recurring revenue, strategic fit – before a line of code is written.

Design Principle

Start lean. Structure the data correctly from day one.

Each platform begins as a focused wedge into one painful workflow – and is built so the system underneath can support the dashboards, reporting, and scale that come later.
