Pillar 01 · SLT Growth Advisors

A referral network that treats trust as infrastructure

SLT Growth Advisors connects trusted referrers with hand-selected service partners that need qualified business opportunities – recurring revenue built on relationships, without heavy delivery responsibility.

Refer an Opportunity Become a Service Partner

Operating Model

Every opportunity travels the same path.

From the first introduction to the final payment, each deal is tied to a referrer, a service partner, a fee arrangement, and a payment history.

  1. 01

    A trusted referrer spots an opportunity – a business that needs something a partner in the network does well.

  2. 02

    SLT reviews and qualifies it. Not everything moves forward; the network stays curated on both sides.

  3. 03

    The opportunity is matched with an approved service partner whose ideal client it actually fits.

  4. 04

    The partner delivers the work and revenue is collected against the agreed fee arrangement.

  5. 05

    The referrer is paid their share – tracked, documented, and visible.

  6. 06

    SLT retains its margin, and the outcome feeds back into what the network looks for next.

What Participants Can Expect

Managed like a curated sales channel, not a passive contact list.

CONTROLS

Every deal accounted for

Each opportunity is tied to a referrer, a service partner, a fee arrangement, and a payment history. Ownership of an introduction is never ambiguous.

TRANSPARENCY

Visible status, documents, payments

Referrers and partners see appropriate deal status, documents, and payment records – so nobody has to ask where things stand.

ENGAGEMENT

A network kept thinking

Regular updates on success stories, ideal client profiles, and new capabilities make it easy for referrers to recognize opportunities in the wild.
